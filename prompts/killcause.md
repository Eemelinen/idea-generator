## Kill-cause brief

Your only job is to find reasons this idea **fails**. Someone else researches the upside.

- **Dead competitors:** businesses in Finland that tried this or something close and closed. Local or hybrid: the base's municipality, region and nearby towns first. Online or hybrid: webshops and marketplace sellers in the target markets. Search the PRH data for dissolved or deregistered companies in the relevant TOL industry, then look for news or forum posts that explain why they closed.
- **Free substitutes:** free, DIY, public-sector or bundled alternatives that make paying unnecessary; for online ideas also large marketplaces and international webshops that already ship to the target markets.
- **Regulatory blockers:** permits, zoning, food or safety rules, or licensing that could make the idea impossible or uneconomic at the base; for online ideas also distance-selling, consumer-protection and product rules in the target markets. Cite official domains.
- **"Why hasn't this been done here?"** Look for the structural reason: population, distance, seasonality, cost; for online ideas, shipping costs and times, platform fees and price competition.

Set kill-trigger flags only when a quote directly supports them. A missing source is a gap, not a kill trigger.
