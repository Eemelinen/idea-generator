## Kill-cause brief

Your only job is to find reasons this idea **fails**. Someone else researches the upside.

- **Dead competitors:** businesses in Finland (Rääkkylä and North Karelia first) that tried this or something close and closed. Search the PRH data for dissolved or deregistered companies in the relevant TOL industry, then look for news or forum posts that explain why they closed.
- **Free substitutes:** free, DIY, public-sector or bundled alternatives that make paying unnecessary.
- **Regulatory blockers:** permits, zoning, food or safety rules, or licensing that could make the idea impossible or uneconomic here. Cite official domains.
- **"Why hasn't this been done here?"** Look for the structural reason: population, distance, seasonality, cost.

Set kill-trigger flags only when a quote directly supports them. A missing source is a gap, not a kill trigger.
